Starting
New here? How this works
  1. Open My game and pick your name. It shows your next match and which court to go to.
  2. Draw shows who plays whom. Live shows what is on each court right now.
  3. When a match ends, tap it and type the score. The Rankings update by themselves.
  4. Organisers set everything up under Manage.
Knockout
Lose once and you are out.
Round robin
Everyone plays everyone. Most wins tops the table.
Pool
A small group inside a division. The best in each pool move on.
Bye
A free pass to the next round because there is no opponent.
Walkover
A win because the opponent did not play.
Seed
A starting rank, so the strongest players do not meet too early.
